# Snapshot Finance - 30 Second Instagram Reel Script

**Target Audience:** Australians (25-45) juggling multiple financial accounts, possibly with a partner.
**Goal:** Clearly communicate the USP (all statements, no linking) and drive uploads.
**Tone:** Energetic, clear, and highly visual.

---

| Time | Visual (Vertical 9:16) | On-Screen Text (Bold & Clear) | Audio (Voiceover & SFX) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 - 0:05** | Fast cuts of different financial documents: A credit card statement, a partner's bank statement, a personal bank account PDF on a laptop. A person looks overwhelmed. | **Your subscriptions are EVERYWHERE.** | *(Upbeat, catchy synth track starts)* <br> **VO:** "Your subscriptions are spread across your credit card, your partner's account, your own bank... It's impossible to track!" |
| **0:05 - 0:12** | **[Screen Recording]** <br> The user drags and drops THREE different PDF files (labeled 'Credit Card.pdf', 'Partner.pdf', 'My-Account.pdf') into the Snapshot Finance upload box. <br> A big "NO ACCOUNT LINKING REQUIRED" stamp animates over the screen. | **Upload ALL your statements.** <br> **NO account linking.** | *(Swoosh SFX for each drop)* <br> **VO:** "Forget linking bank accounts. Just upload ALL your statements. Yes, even your credit card and your partner's account." *(Sound of a secure lock clicking)* |
| **0:12 - 0:18** | **[Product Animation]** <br> The three documents fly into the Snapshot logo and a single, unified 'Finish Page' report scrolls out. The 'Total Yearly Spend' number is highlighted dramatically. | **One simple view.** | **VO:** "Our AI instantly combines them into one simple, secure snapshot. See your TRUE total yearly spend." |
| **0:18 - 0:25** | **[Product Shot]** <br> The unified report is shown. A finger scrolls and taps on a "forgotten" subscription (e.g., an annual software renewal from the credit card statement). A red circle highlights it. | **Find what you're REALLY spending.** | *(Cha-ching SFX)* <br> **VO:** "Finally, see what you're *really* spending and spot those forgotten subscriptions bleeding you dry." |
| **0:25 - 0:30** | **[Call to Action]** <br> Clean, full-screen logo. <br> A clear "Link in Bio" sticker animation points up. | **Get Your FREE Snapshot.** <br> **Link in Bio!** | *(Music swells and finishes)* <br> **VO:** "Stop wasting money. Get your free, secure preview now. Link in bio!" |

---

**Key Selling Points to Emphasize:**
*   **Multi-Account:** Show different types of statements.
*   **No Linking:** This is a major trust and security selling point. Make it big and bold.
*   **Consolidation:** The visual of multiple docs becoming one report is key.
*   **Action:** Clear "Link in Bio" call to action for Instagram.
